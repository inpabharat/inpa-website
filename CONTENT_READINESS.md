# Content-readiness checklist

The site foundation intentionally does not guess missing institutional or scientific facts. Every item below blocks replacement of the corresponding visible development placeholder.

- [ ] Official INPA logo and brand usage guidance.
- [ ] Confirmed legal/official organisation name and abbreviation styling.
- [ ] Approved domain and ownership contact.
- [ ] President's name, title, professional photograph and approved message.
- [ ] Approved About copy.
- [ ] Current Executive Council roster, roles, terms and affiliations.
- [ ] Current Constitution file and publication status.
- [ ] Office address and verified contact channels.
- [ ] Official social-media URLs.
- [ ] Membership process and destination URL.
- [ ] Donations information or approved destination, if shown.
- [ ] Nuclear Horizons latest issue, cover, editorial, archive and submission route.
- [ ] Verified launch news and events.
- [ ] Featured-research selection, citation and publication permissions.
- [ ] Student resources, opportunity owners and review dates.
- [ ] Verified institution/map dataset and official links.
- [ ] Distinguished-scientist profiles and member-highlight approvals.
- [ ] Scientific imagery, credits, licences and alternative text.
- [ ] Privacy Policy and content-correction contact.

## Launch rule

Development fixtures must not be migrated to production. Placeholder routes and sections must remain `noindex` or be replaced with approved content before launch. No generated or generic image may be presented as a real Indian facility, experiment, scientist or event.
