The modified district database at /maps/districts.json is made available under ODbL 1.0. This licence applies to that boundary database, not the INPA application or institution directory.

Modifications: The geoBoundaries simplified GeoJSON geometry is further simplified at a tolerance of 0.003 degrees, projected with the same generalized coordinate mapping as the institution markers, and clipped for display to the existing Survey of India national outline. Labels use the centroid of the largest polygon and are omitted where they would overlap markers or other labels.
Generation script: scripts/prepare-map-boundaries.mjs in https://github.com/inpabharat/inpa-website

These are generalized historical reference layers, not a current official administrative inventory or a precise geodetic map. No claim is made that every recent state or district reorganisation is represented. Source coordinates and institution markers are approximate.
